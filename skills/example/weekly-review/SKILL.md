# Weekly review

## Before you start

Read the last 10 entries in `LEARNINGS.md` in this folder and apply them.

## Steps

1. Gather the week's meeting transcripts, call records, and open tasks from the SecondBrain data folder.
2. Summarize decisions, commitments, and open questions.
3. List follow-ups, each with an owner and a due date.

## After you finish

Append one entry to `LEARNINGS.md` in this folder: what worked, and what to change next time.
